## Worker mathematics (frozen heuristic)

For candidate w in the positive integer grid1..20, anchor a is own most recent
actual contracted salary. If w<=a, alpha/beta=2/1; otherwise1/4. For own comparable
wins/losses s/f, `p_num=alpha+s`, `p_den=alpha+beta+s+f`. Retain source IDs and
unknown exclusions. These priors are fallible modeling assumptions; do not fit
them to outcomes, infer rivals' bids or treat them as true market probabilities.

Use actual held cash at15:55. First project today's remaining own bread/ale
basket using personally known quotes and held food; do not add late income or a
prospective wage before its start. This common prelude is affordability-only,
not a real purchase or stock reservation. From that remaining cash project three
next-day household days, wages arriving at the genuine industry work-end (all
before16:00), one bread/day and at most one ale/day. Goods availability is assumed
**only** for labeled conditional affordability. Existing basket utility selects
each day's basket:100 bread +5 ale + remaining coins, with existing cash ties.
Carry actual modeled coins and held food forward. Total horizon value is sum of
consumption benefits plus terminal cash (retained coins counted once, not once
per day). Baseline has no new wages, does not monetize unpaid claims or assume
an unoffered renewal. Conditional value gain is U(w)-U(0).

Compare exact rational `p_num*gain/p_den`, require strictly positive. Ties prefer
larger w, then stable visible notice ID. Same conditional budget for w is reused
across personally visible firms because their existing wage windows all precede
shopping; only own firm-specific chance evidence differs. Record baseline,
20 wage budgets with three day rows, per-notice priors/source IDs/exact scores,
selected ask/reason, current information and unavailable facts. No Cora Bayesian
forecast, personality variation, forced underbid or subsistence floor.
